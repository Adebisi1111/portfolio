About

I'm a Nigerian Web3 builder and computer science student at the University of Ilorin. I work at the edge of autonomous finance — designing agents that hold balances, execute scheduled payments, and interact with on-chain protocols without constant human input.

As a Circle Arc Architect, I build educational content and working demos that show what developers can actually ship on a stablecoin-native chain: agent wallets, nanopayments, treasury apps, and programmable money flows.

My hands-on projects span Circle Arc (Concord autonomous agent) and Robinhood Chain (mint automation), with a focus on real transactions, not simulations.

Circle Arc · Autonomous Agent

Concord — Autonomous Payment Agent

A self-custody Arc agent wallet that pays multiple recipients (by Gmail, not address) in USDC or EURC on a set schedule. Real on-chain transfers, transaction history, per-user wallets, and a VPS cron that re-settles daily from each wallet's balance.
